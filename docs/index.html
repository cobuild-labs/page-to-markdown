Made for working with AI

Give your AI the whole page, not a messy paste.

Markdown is the format AI tools read best. It keeps the structure (headings, lists, tables, who said what) and drops the HTML and clutter that waste tokens and confuse the model.

Feed real data into your analysis

Turn a 10-page thread, a long directory or a pile of reviews into one file, then ask Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or NotebookLM about all of it.

Reach pages your AI can't open

Pages behind your login, built with JavaScript or split over many pages are read in your own browser, exactly as you see them.

Hand work from one AI to another

Export a chat with every search, script and result behind it, so the next chat picks up where the last one left off.

Learn from videos faster

Save a lecture's captions as clean paragraphs, then ask an AI to summarise them, make notes or quiz you.

Spend fewer tokens

A cleaned-up file is a fraction of the raw page's size, so more of what matters fits in the context window.

And when you're not using AI

Save articles to Obsidian or Notion, archive a whole discussion in order, or keep a readable copy that won't break like a bookmark.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download the latest zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu, then press Alt Shift M on any page.
