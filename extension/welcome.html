Page to Markdown is ready

Turn any web page, a whole multi-page forum thread, or an AI chat into one clean Markdown file. Everything happens in your browser.

1
Pin it to your toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Page to Markdown.
📄

Any page

Click the icon and choose Convert or Copy. Highlight text first to convert just that part.

📚

Whole threads

"Convert a multi-page thread" finds every page and combines them. Forum posts get their author, date and quotes.

🤖

AI chats

On Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok, export the chat with the thinking, every tool it ran and the sources, ready to hand to another AI.

⚙️

Your way

File name patterns, Obsidian-style front matter, copy instead of download, dark mode. All in Settings.

Keyboard shortcuts

Open Page to MarkdownAlt + Shift + K
Convert this pageAlt + Shift + M
Export the open AI chatAlt + Shift + E
Right-click any page or selectionConvert page / Convert selection

Change them any time at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Your privacy

🔒

No account, no tracking, no analytics, no servers. Pages and chats are read inside your own browser tab and saved straight to your computer. The only thing stored is your settings.
